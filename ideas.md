# ME Lighting — Design Specification

## Reference and Chosen Direction

The user-supplied `METheme.png` is the ground-truth visual reference. The implementation will preserve its premium residential-lighting character, dense but orderly landing-page structure, dramatic low-key interior imagery, black-and-white foundation, and restrained warm-gold accents. Fidelity to the reference takes priority over introducing a different visual concept.

## Design Movement

**Contemporary architectural luxury**, borrowing from high-end lighting catalogues, residential architecture portfolios, and precision engineering brands.

## Core Principles

1. **Light is the material:** illuminated coves, downlights, pendants, and landscape lighting are the primary visual storytelling devices.
2. **Confident contrast:** near-black surfaces and warm white space alternate to establish a strong editorial rhythm.
3. **Precision over decoration:** lines, spacing, icons, and buttons remain crisp, functional, and minimally ornamented.
4. **Guided decisions:** every section helps a homeowner move from inspiration toward the right solution or an expert consultation.

## Color Philosophy

Near-black communicates expertise and architectural seriousness; warm white keeps informational sections approachable; **ME Gold** adds warmth and recalls the colour temperature of premium residential lighting. Gold is used selectively for emphasis, never as a broad decorative wash.

## Layout Paradigm

A full-width, cinematic hero establishes atmosphere, followed by horizontally composed solution cards and alternating light/dark editorial bands. Desktop sections use broad, low profiles and clear lateral movement; mobile collapses these into tactile carousels or stacked rows without losing hierarchy.

## Signature Elements

- Warm-gold illuminated rules and dots, echoing concealed LED strips.
- Crisp white line icons that identify spaces and benefits.
- Dark image cards with controlled bottom gradients and architectural crops.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel immediate and assured: gold underline travel on navigation, subtle image enlargement on cards, and short press feedback on buttons. Calls to action lead to the contact section or open a lightweight project brief form; no dead-end controls.

## Animation

Use opacity and small vertical translations for section entrances, stagger card reveals by 45–60ms, and keep hover transitions within 220ms. Image zoom should be subtle. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid decorative continuous animation.

## Typography System

Use **Manrope** for bold architectural headlines and interface labels, paired with **DM Sans** for body copy and metadata. Headlines are compact, weight 750–800, and tightly tracked; eyebrow labels are uppercase, gold, and widely tracked. Body copy maintains generous line-height and moderate measure.

## Brand Essence

**Expert lighting guidance and premium solutions for Australian homes, delivered with confidence from plan to installation.** Personality: assured, practical, refined.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and outcome-led. CTAs sound helpful and decisive; microcopy removes uncertainty without overselling.

Examples: **“Lighting. Solved.”** and **“Bring us the plan. We’ll bring it to life.”**

## Wordmark & Logo

The website now uses the client-supplied gold-and-deep-red geometric light-beam emblem paired with its metallic gold **ME LIGHTING** wordmark. The original textured dark background and “COMMERCIAL LED” tagline are removed, leaving a clean transparent horizontal logo for the header and footer.

## Signature Brand Color

**ME Gold — `#C9A35C`**, a warm metallic amber chosen to evoke 2700K architectural lighting without feeling yellow or ornamental.

## Page Structure

1. Slim black navigation with wordmark, primary links, and outlined “Upload your plans” action.
2. Low-key residential-interior hero with left-aligned headline, proof-oriented copy, primary plan CTA, and expert-advice text link.
3. Warm-white solutions section with six image cards: New Home, Kitchen, Bathroom, Outdoor, Feature Lighting, Whole Home.
4. Black “Why choose ME Lighting?” band with five concise trust pillars.
5. Knowledge Centre editorial row with an introduction and four article cards.
6. Dark architectural-plan CTA with concise project brief and upload/contact action.
7. Four practical outcomes in a light reassurance strip.
8. Deep-black footer with brand statement, grouped links, and verified ME Lighting contact information.

## Content Integrity

Use documented company facts: established in 2003, LED lighting design/supply/installation, indoor and garden/landscape specialisation, Sydney and Mittagong contact details, and genuine award history. Do not fabricate reviews, customer counts, product claims, or testimonial quotations.

## Style Decisions

- Supporting imagery always presents lighting as the subject: warm architectural illumination, shadow, coves, downlights, pendants, steps, or landscape effects. Bright generic room photography is excluded.
- ME Gold `#C9A35C` is a precision accent for illuminated rules, dots, underlines, key icons, and primary actions; broad gold washes and decorative gradients are avoided.
- Cards are treated as architectural catalogue plates: image-led, crisp, low-ornament, and editorial rather than generic rounded service or blog tiles.
- The contact form is presented as a structured project consultation, with dark architectural surfaces, fine linework, and clear next-step hierarchy.

## Selling Points Reference Update

The user supplied a reference featuring a bold photographic introduction panel followed by tall benefit cards that overlap the image area. For ME Lighting, this composition is translated into the established architectural-luxury system rather than copied literally: black cinematic photography replaces the blue background, warm-white cards replace pastel fills, ME Gold provides precise rules and icon accents, and every panel uses straight corners. The six cards use oversized value statements, supporting copy, numbered hierarchy, and an architectural-lighting image anchored in each lower panel. Desktop uses a three-by-two gallery; mobile becomes a horizontal snap rail so each card retains impact and legibility.

## Hero Form and Best Sellers Reference Update

The revised page order is hero with an embedded compact project-enquiry form, then the ME Lighting Advantage selling-points stage as the second section. A new Best Sellers section follows, combining the product taxonomy shown in the supplied dark product-grid reference with the centred carousel hierarchy shown in the supplied tiling reference: one enlarged active product card, partially visible and quieter neighbouring cards, direct arrows, and precise pagination. Straight corners, disciplined gold accents, and low-glare materials remain mandatory.

This card band reads as the second visual section while remaining attached to the hero. The first four sections are fixed as: hero and form, elevated USP band, continuously moving solutions carousel, and Best Sellers carousel.
